Set up a school — your course website

How to set up a school in WHITEBOARD — details and URL, cover image, design template, visibility, and publishing

In this guide you'll set up a school — a branded course site that showcases all your courses to students and the wider public. By the end, you'll have a live site with its own URL, ready to sell.

1Open My knowledge hubs

In the side menu, click My knowledge hubs. The Schools card at the top of the screen shows how many schools you already have and their status.

2Create a new school

Create a new school from the knowledge hubs screen. Once created, you'll land on the school's management screen, with tabs for Layout, Settings, Brand kit, My students, Email campaigns, and Analytics.

Good to know

How many course sites you can create depends on your plan — up to 5 course sites on the Educator plan, unlimited on the Leader plan. Full details in WHITEBOARD plans.

3Fill in the school details

In the Settings tab, under School details, fill in:

  1. School name — up to 80 characters.
  2. Description — up to 300 characters. Shown to students on the school page.
  3. URL — the unique address where students will find your school, in the format whiteboard.education/schools/....

4Upload a cover image

Under Cover image, upload the main image that appears at the top of your school. We recommend 1280×720 pixels, at a 16:9 ratio.

5Pick a design template

Under School template, choose the style your school will have for students. There are several ready-made templates — see the full comparison in School design templates.

Pro tip

Want full control over colors, fonts, and logo? Set all of those in the Brand kit tab, with a live preview of both the school page and the invitation email.

6Set visibility

Under Visibility, choose who can see the school: Public — open to everyone, or Private — invite only.

7Edit the pages in edit mode

Open the school itself. As an instructor, you see it in edit mode: a top bar reads "Edit mode — school", and you can drag courses to reorder them and click any card to edit it. The Preview button shows how the site looks to a student, and the student/instructor toggle in the corner switches between the two views.

8Publish

When everything's ready, click Publish changes in the editing bar. Your school goes live at the URL you set.

Heads-up

Deleting a school is irreversible — all the data, courses, and students attached to it are permanently deleted. The button is at the bottom of the Settings tab.

What's next

Now set up the store: Your school's course store — course cards, tags, and prices — and round out the info pages.

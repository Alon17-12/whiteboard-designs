Events and meetups in the club

The club's event calendar — Zoom meetups, in-person workshops, registration, and recordings of past sessions

A good club doesn't happen only on screen — there are Zoom meetups, in-person workshops, and Q&A sessions. In the events area you see everything on one calendar, register with a click, and catch up on recordings of anything you missed.

1Open the events area

In the club's side navigation rail, click the events icon. At the top of the screen there are three tabs: Calendar, List, and Recordings.

2Look at the calendar

In the Calendar view, every event is marked on its day, color-coded by type: online meetup, in-person meetup, or recording. Move between months with the navigation arrows.

3Register for an event

On the side of the screen you'll see Upcoming events — each one with its date, time, location (Zoom or a physical address), and an Online / In person tag. Click Register to save your spot.

Pro tip

Turning on Event reminders in the notification preferences of your profile will make sure you never miss a meetup.

4Watch recordings of past meetups

Missed a session? The Recordings tab gathers the recordings of meetups that already happened — jump in and watch whenever it suits you.

What's next

Between meetups, the conversation keeps going in the club chat.

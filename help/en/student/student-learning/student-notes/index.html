My notes — timestamped notes

Writing notes while you watch, jumping back to the exact moment in the video, and exporting a notebook to PDF

A note written at the right moment is worth gold when you review the material. On WHITEBOARD, every note you write during a lesson is tied to that point in the video — click it later and you jump straight to the moment you wrote it.

1Write a note during a lesson

In the course player, click the notes button on the side rail. The My notes panel opens — write your note and click Save note. The note is saved together with the video's current timestamp.

2Open My summaries

All your notes from all your courses are gathered on the My summaries screen (in the side menu). Each note appears as a card with the course name, the lesson, a content preview — and a timestamp.

3Jump to the right moment

Clicking the timestamp on a note (say, 14:32) opens the lesson and jumps the video to that exact point. No need to remember "where did they say that" — the note remembers for you.

Pro tip

Write a quick note whenever the instructor says something worth coming back to. Even two words are enough — the timestamp does the rest.

4Filter and search

At the top of the screen you can filter (All / By course), search inside your notes, and sort by date or course.

5View a tidy notebook and export to PDF

In the By course view, your notes arrange themselves like a notebook — by chapters and lessons, with timestamps next to each summary. From there you can click Export notebook to PDF and get a clean file of all your summaries in the course, with the option to include chapter headings and timestamps.

What's next

Build an exam

Build a multiple-choice exam step by step — questions, correct answer, passing grade, time limit, and video references for wrong answers

An exam in WHITEBOARD checks that your students actually understood the material — and can even block progress in the course until they pass. In this guide you'll build a complete exam: questions, grading settings, and a smart reference back to the lesson for when a student gets it wrong.

1Open the exam editor

Enter the exam from the course structure in the knowledge hub. At the top of the screen you'll see the Edit mode bar with a Draft status, an Auto-saved indicator, and a Publish button. You edit the exam name right in the title.

2Set the exam rules

In the settings bar, you define how the exam behaves:

  • Passing grade — drag the slider to the percentage you want.
  • Blocking exam — students can't continue the course until they pass.
  • Retakes — unlimited, or 1 to 5.
  • Show answers — whether students see the correct answers at the end.
  • Shuffle questions — random question order for each student.
  • Time limit — turn it on and set the minutes.

3Write the questions

Click Add question, type the question text and the answer options. Mark the correct answer by clicking the circle next to it. You can add an option with Add option, delete an option (at least 2 are required), drag questions to reorder, and delete an entire question.

4Point wrong answers to a lesson clip

For every question you can set a lesson reference: pick a lesson from the course and type a timestamp (min:sec). When a student gets the question wrong, instead of just seeing the correct answer — they watch the relevant clip from the lesson that explains it. Watch clip lets you make sure you picked the right moment.

Pro tip

Video references turn an exam from measurement into learning — the student who got it wrong goes right back to the exact point they missed.

5Review and publish

Preview shows the exam from the student's point of view. When everything's ready, click Publish — the status changes from Draft to Published and the exam is available to students. Your changes are saved automatically the whole time you're editing.

Heads-up

Deleting a question is immediate and can't be undone. If you're on the fence — better to edit the wording than to delete.

What the student sees

Students reach the exam from their learning path and get an opening screen: the number of questions, the passing grade, and the time limit — and if the exam is blocking, a clear warning: "You must pass this exam to advance to the next lesson". They click Start exam and answer question by question, with a progress counter, a timer, and Previous / Next navigation. Before submitting there's a confirmation — after submitting, there's no going back.

At the end, students see their grade in a big ring with a full question review — their answer next to the correct one. Passed? A "Next lesson unlocked" banner and a To the next lesson button. Failed? They see how many attempts remain, which lessons are worth revisiting, and on every wrong question you set a reference for — a Watch the explanation from the lesson card that plays the exact clip.

What's next

Students have started taking the exam? Head to Exam results — the instructor's view. Want to award a certificate at the end? Certificates — templates and issuing.

Certificates — templates and issuing

Pick a certificate template, set a signature and issuing conditions — and see what students get when they finish

A completion certificate gives students a reason to make it to the end — and something to be proud of afterward. In this guide you'll pick a template, customize the content, and decide when the certificate is issued automatically.

1Open the certificate tab

Enter the knowledge hub and switch to the Certificate tab. The screen we saw in the design belongs to a training program, alongside tabs like Layout, Settings, and Email campaigns.

2Choose a template

The Choose certificate template gallery has four templates: Classic, Modern, Professional, and Creative. Your brand kit — colors and logo — is injected automatically into every template, and a live preview on the side updates with each choice.

3Customize the certificate content

In the Certificate settings card, you set:

  • Custom text — the sentence that appears above the student's name (for example, "Proudly awarded to:").
  • Instructor signature — drag in a signature image or type the instructor's name.

4Set the issuing conditions

Check off what's required for the certificate to be issued: Completed all courses in the program, Passed all exams, and you can also add Attended all one-on-one meetings. You can also turn on Include verification QR — a QR code on the certificate that lets anyone verify it's authentic.

Click Save certificate settings, and Full preview shows the certificate at full size.

What the student sees

When a student meets the conditions, they get a celebratory screen with their certificate: name, issue date, serial number, signature, and QR. From there they can Download PDF, share on LinkedIn or WhatsApp, and see a summary of what they completed in the program.

Good to know

Every certificate carries a unique serial number, so even without the QR, any issued certificate can be identified.

What's next

The issuing conditions lean on exams — make sure yours are built right in Building an exam. The overall look is set in the course brand kit.
